# Quantum Market Sentinel

Elegant futuristic dashboard monitoring TSLA, NVDA, SEV (Aptera Motors), PLTR, AMZN, and SPCX (SpaceX).

## Features
- Real-time-style stock cards with prices, market caps, and key metrics (data as of ~Oct 15, 2026)
- Composite sentiment scores with visual bars
- Relevant news snippets that could influence market swings
- Balanced investment play recommendations based on fundamentals and news (not financial advice)
- Innovative **Quantum Sentiment Constellation** visualization: glowing orbs sized by scale, colored by sentiment, with connecting filaments and central news pulse
- Dark sci-fi aesthetic, responsive, animated

## Innovative Visualization
The Quantum Sentiment Constellation places companies as neural orbs in a circular layout. Orb size reflects relative scale, hue maps to sentiment energy (bullish green / neutral yellow / bearish red), and animated connections suggest correlation structure. The central pulse represents aggregated news flow.

**Sentiment Energy Formula** (illustrative):
\[ SE = 0.35 \cdot Momentum + 0.40 \cdot NewsPolarity + 0.25 \cdot FundamentalScore \]
normalized to 0-100, modulated by volatility.
